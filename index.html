[Explore Programs](#programs) [Apply Now](#admissions) Cour d’honneur, Paris campus ## A University Without Borders Since 1887, Université Aurelia has been a place where people from very different countries learn to work on the same questions. Our 34,000 students come from 142 countries and study in French and English across seven faculties and schools. Classes are small enough for real discussion. Research is close enough to the curriculum that undergraduates can join a laboratory in their second year. We measure success by what our graduates do next: the companies they found, the papers they publish, and the communities they build in cities far from Paris and Lyon. #### Academic excellence Teaching led by active researchers, with rigorous, reviewed programmes. #### Global community Students and faculty from every continent, in every classroom. #### Innovation Incubators, industry partners and open labs on both campuses. ## Academic Programs Four levels of study, one consistent standard. Every program is taught by faculty who are also active researchers. ### Undergraduate Three-year Licence degrees with a broad first year, then a focused specialisation and a study-abroad option.
