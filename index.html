A University Without Borders

Since 1887, Université Aurelia has been a place where people from very different countries learn to work on the same questions.

Our 34,000 students come from 142 countries and study in French and English across seven faculties and schools. Classes are small enough for real discussion. Research is close enough to the curriculum that undergraduates can join a laboratory in their second year.

We measure success by what our graduates do next: the companies they found, the papers they publish, and the communities they build in cities far from Paris and Lyon.

A Global Community in the Heart of Europe

One in three Aurelia students arrives from abroad, and every student can spend a semester somewhere else in the world.

International students

Students from 142 countries, with a dedicated welcome team from visa to first week.

Exchange programs

Campus Life

Between lectures, our campuses are places to meet, make things and rest. Most of it is run by students.

The courtyard is our living room

Markets, concerts and open-air lectures take place here from April to October.

Student clubs & organizations

More than 180 groups, from robotics to debate to film. Starting a new one takes a signature and a semester plan.

Arts & culture

Theatre, choir, orchestra and gallery space, open to every student regardless of degree.

Sports

Events

Weekly lectures, career evenings and cultural festivals.

Cafés

Three student-run cafés with affordable meals all day.

Libraries & study spaces
